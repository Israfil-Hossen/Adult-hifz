# Screenshots — you have to take these on the phone

Play needs **at least 2** phone screenshots (up to 8). I could not produce them
from here: they have to come from a real device to be honest about how the app
actually looks, and Play prefers device captures.

Install `app-debug.apk` (or this bundle via internal testing), then capture:

| # | Screen | Why it earns a slot |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **আজ / Today** with a few pages memorised | the mushaf map filling in, the day's lesson, the habit row |
| 2 | **The mushaf page** with today's lines marked | the real page, true line breaks — this is the product |
| 3 | **শুধু শুনুন / Listen** on the সূরা tab | shows it is usable by someone who only listens |
| 4 | **অগ্রগতি / Progress** | the juz bars and the heat map |
| 5 | The ✔ having just marked a page | optional, but it is the new feature |

Requirements: PNG or JPEG, 16:9 or 9:16, each side between 320 px and 3840 px.
A 1080x1920 portrait capture is the safe choice.

Put them in this folder named `01-today.png`, `02-mushaf.png`, and so on.
